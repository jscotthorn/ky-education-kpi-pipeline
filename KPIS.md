## Kindergarten Readiness
**Source**: `etl/kindergarten_readiness.py`

KDE's BRIGANCE kindergarten screen publishes the share of screened students in each readiness group. `Total Ready` is the percent ready (ready plus enrichments). `Ready With Interventions` is the not-ready share. The three component percents are shares of screened students. They sometimes sum to 99 or 101 because each cell is rounded.

On the KYRC24, KYRC25, and KYRC26 composites the columns are named like counts and the cells are those percents. Each rate is the published percent. `kindergarten_readiness_rate` is `Total Ready`. The component rates and the prior-setting rates are the published cells too. Those years do not emit `_count` or `_total` metrics, because those names were holding percents. The stored value is the published number, so a component sum of 99 or 101 does not move the rate. The 2026 state row is 55 / 38 / 7 / 45, and the readiness rate is 45. This replaces a rate that divided by the sum of the rounded components. Existing KYRC24 values change by up to 0.86 points on All Students readiness rates (215 cells) and by up to 0.98 points on other rate cells. Consumers of `kindergarten_readiness_rate` — `analysis/scripts/kindergarten_readiness_analysis.py`, `analysis/bayesian_models/kindergarten_readiness_model.py`, the dashboard, and `scripts/generate_report_drafts.py` — will see those small changes.

On the 2018-2021 files, `kindergarten_readiness_rate` is the published total-ready percent and `kindergarten_readiness_total` is the published number tested. `kindergarten_readiness_count` is `round(percent / 100 * tested)`. KDE publishes the percent and the tested count, not that headcount, so the count is an estimate. It is exact when the tested count is below about 1,000, and about +/-24 students at state level, because the percent is rounded to one decimal. 2022 and 2023 publish the percent and do not publish a tested count, so they have a rate and no total. Their prior-setting percents are not stored. The 2020-23 component columns and that 2022-23 tested-count gap are issue #90.

State rows are district `All Districts` for 2024-2026 and district `State` for 2018-2023. The 2026 state All Students / prior setting All Students figures are 55 / 38 / 7 / 45. An unweighted mean of the district totals is not the state figure and is not stored.

A `*` is value NA with suppressed Y. A blank row (not Y, every measure empty) is not stored. A published 0 is kept. A 2020 row with NUMBER TESTED 0 and a blank percent stores that total and does not invent a rate. In 2020-2023 KDE publishes no 0 and no *; suppression is a blank cell with SUPPRESSED Y. Most percent cells are blank, for example 49,666 of 65,351 in 2020, so a Y in those years can mean 0-2 or not available. A 2020-21 All Students prior row that is suppressed Y, with a published NUMBER TESTED and a blank percent, stores the rate and the count as value NA with suppressed Y. The tested count on that Y row is also stored as NA. That stored-as-NA total predates the rate and count rows. The 2018-19 workbook `Y` flag means some domain cell is suppressed; a published composite percent on that row is kept.

2018-19 co-op totals are `---COOP Total---` under the published co-op code 901-909. 2017-18 publishes the same nine totals as `---District Total---` with a blank district name and school code 901-909. Those rows are stored with the 2019 labels: the long co-op name, `---COOP Total---`, and the abbreviation. Greater Louisville (909) has no abbreviation.

`KYRC23_ASMT_Kindergarten_Screen_Composite.csv` republishes 2022-23 and is not loaded. Skipping it drops three 2023 groups only that file publishes: Non Economically Disadvantaged, Non English Learner, and Student without Disabilities (IEP). That is 15,126 source rows. Issue #95. `kindergarten_screen_2023.csv` has 91 Breathitt County rows whose school year is `20022023`. The year taken from that field is 2023. Issue #91. No code change.

The 2018-19 coversheet says demographic `ACD` is Disabilities (IEP). Those rows are Students with Disabilities (IEP). The 2019 state disability row is 6,104 tested and 34.2 percent ready.

## Out-of-School Suspension
**Source**: `etl/out_of_school_suspension.py`

KDE's report-card glossary defines `OVW_Student_Suspensions` as Student Suspensions (CRDC): counts of in-school and out-of-school suspensions for students with and without disabilities, from the biennial Civil Rights Data Collection. Single means one out-of-school suspension. Multiple means more than one. These are student counts.

**The KPI year is the report-card year (`School Year`), not the CRDC collected year.** Report-card year 2026 is the 2021-22 collection (`Collected School Year` `20212022` on every KYRC26 row). Report-card year 2024 is the 2020-21 collection (`Collected School Year` `20202021` on every KYRC24 row). The output has no collected-year column.

`KYRC25_OVW_Student_Suspensions.csv` is the same 2020-21 collection with `School Year` `20242025`. It is not registered. These files are school rows only. There is no `999000` state row and no district total.

A total is suppressed when any component is suppressed. Summing the published parts is not the file's count, including the case where every part is starred and the sum of the remaining parts is 0.

Annual Safe Schools `OUT OF SCHOOL SUSPENSION SSP3` counts are not emitted here. `etl/safe_schools_discipline.py` publishes `out_of_school_suspension_count` and the other resolution counts.

### CRDC metrics (report-card years 2024 and 2026)
- `out_of_school_suspension_single_with_disabilities_count` - Students with disabilities with one out-of-school suspension
- `out_of_school_suspension_single_without_disabilities_count` - Students without disabilities with one out-of-school suspension
- `out_of_school_suspension_multiple_with_disabilities_count` - Students with disabilities with more than one out-of-school suspension
- `out_of_school_suspension_multiple_without_disabilities_count` - Students without disabilities with more than one out-of-school suspension
- `out_of_school_suspension_single_total_count` - Single-suspension students, both disability groups. Suppressed when either component is suppressed
- `out_of_school_suspension_multiple_total_count` - Multiple-suspension students, both disability groups. Suppressed when either component is suppressed
- `out_of_school_suspension_total_count` - Students with an out-of-school suspension (single plus multiple). Suppressed when any component is suppressed
- `in_school_suspension_with_disabilities_count` - In-school suspensions for students with disabilities
- `in_school_suspension_without_disabilities_count` - In-school suspensions for students without disabilities
- `in_school_suspension_total_count` - In-school suspensions, both disability groups. Suppressed when either component is suppressed

## Safe Schools Events
**Source**: `etl/safe_schools_events.py`

The stored count is the cell KDE published. A count of KPI rows is a count of stored cells, not a figure KDE publishes. `All Students` is the unduplicated student count (`safe_students_affected_*`). `Total Events` is the event count (`safe_event_count_*`, student group `All Students - Total Events`). Other demographic rows are event counts (`safe_event_count_*_by_demo`). Female plus Male equals `Total Events`, not `All Students`. The same metric is not a share of `All Students`. This module does not emit `out_of_school_suspension_count`. Safe Schools Discipline owns that count.

A `*` is value NA with `suppressed` Y, including when every count cell on the row is `*`. A blank cell is not stored. A published 0 is kept with `suppressed` N. The 2020-2026 files publish 0, so a star is not a stand-in for zero. Grade files also publish blank cells for grades a school does not serve. KYRC state rows are district `All Districts` with school code `999000`. The 2020-2023 state rows are district `State` with school code `999`. Those codes are stored as published. `safe_incident_rate_*` is an incident rate: events per involved student. It is Total Events divided by All Students for the same entity, year, file, and category, rounded to 3 decimal places. The pipeline calculates it only when both cells are published numbers and the student count is greater than 0. KDE does not publish it. It is kept as an existing calculation.

The type file owns `safe_students_affected_total` and `safe_event_count_total`. The location and context files publish the same Total. Where the type cell is a number, it matches those files. Where the type cell is `*`, the location Total and the context Total are exactly 3. That is 801 rows in KYRC26, 769 in KYRC25, 775 in KYRC24, and 759 in the KYRC23 republish. The type files never publish 1, 2, or 3. The location, context, and grade files publish 3 and never publish 1 or 2. The starred type Total is stored as value NA with suppressed Y. The location and context 3 is not stored at all. For 110 of the KYRC26 rows, All Grades is also `*`, so that 3 survives in no stored cell. Scott decided on 2026-10-19 to keep that type-file star and not store the location or context Total. The finding stays on [issue #101](https://github.com/jscotthorn/ky-education-kpi-pipeline/issues/101) (kde-recheck, 27 October 2026).

### Students affected (`All Students` rows)
- `safe_students_affected_total` - Students affected by any event
- `safe_students_affected_alcohol`, `safe_students_affected_assault_1st`, `safe_students_affected_drugs`, `safe_students_affected_harassment`, `safe_students_affected_assault_other`, `safe_students_affected_other_state`, `safe_students_affected_tobacco`, `safe_students_affected_weapons`

### Event counts (`Total Events` rows, and `_by_demo` for other groups)
- `safe_event_count_total`
- `safe_event_count_alcohol`, `safe_event_count_assault_1st`, `safe_event_count_drugs`, `safe_event_count_harassment`, `safe_event_count_assault_other`, `safe_event_count_other_state`, `safe_event_count_tobacco`, `safe_event_count_weapons`

### Grade
`safe_students_affected_*` and `safe_event_count_*` for `all_grades`, `preschool`, `kindergarten`, `grade_1` through `grade_12`, and `grade_14`. There is no `grade_13`. KYRC25 and KYRC26 headers `PS`, `K`, `1st`, and `14th` land on those names.

### Location
`classroom`, `bus`, `hallway`, `cafeteria`, `restroom`, `gymnasium`, `playground`, `other_location`, `campus`. The 2020-2023 event-details file also has these columns. This parser reads the type columns from that file. The location and context columns in it are not emitted.

### Context
`school_sponsored_during`, `school_sponsored_after`, `non_school_sponsored_during`, `non_school_after`.

### Incident rate
An incident rate is events per involved student. `safe_incident_rate_total` and the same suffix for each measure above are Total Events divided by All Students for the same entity, year, file, and category, where both cells are published numbers and the student count is greater than 0. A star on either cell produces no rate.

## Safe Schools Discipline
**Source**: `etl/safe_schools_discipline.py`

The parser stores the count KDE published. That is the existing calculation. No rate, share, cross-level sum, or lower-bound total is added. A count of KPI rows is a count of stored published cells, not a figure KDE publishes. `All Students` is the unduplicated student count. Every other demographic row is an event count. The same metric holds both, so a subgroup share is not computed against `All Students`. Female plus Male equals the published `Total Events` row, not `All Students`. That row is skipped by the existing aggregate filter. The parser does not add Female and Male into a replacement. Scott decided on 2026-10-19 to store the published `Total` on `All Students` rows only, as its own metric. `safe_schools_discipline_resolutions_students_unduplicated` is that column on the resolutions file, and the 2020-2023 `TOTAL DISCIPLINE RESOLUTIONS` column. `safe_schools_legal_sanctions_students_unduplicated` is that column on the legal-sanctions file. Each is the unduplicated student count KDE published. Nothing is calculated from it. On a subgroup row `Total` repeats the sum of the event columns, so it is not stored. The previous parser did not emit `Total` and did not sum the components. A published 0 is kept with `suppressed` `N`. A `*` on a row that also has a number is value NA with `suppressed` `Y`. A row whose every count cell is `*` is whole-group suppression and is stored: each cell is value NA with `suppressed` `Y`. Of the 10,321 all-star 2023 rows matched to the KYRC23 republish, 9,057 are all zeros (about 88 percent), 434 have a real positive count (227 of those have out-of-school suspensions above 0, up to 25), and 971 are still starred, so dropping the row would drop those cells. A blank cell is not stored. A row whose count cells are all blank is not stored. The smallest positive number in the 2020-2026 CSVs is 3, and those files also publish 0, so a star in those files is not a stand-in for zero. The 2018-19 workbook publishes 1s and 2s. Its coversheet calls `TOTAL_STUDENTS` an unduplicated student count and `TOTAL_EVENTS` an unduplicated event count, the same split as `All Students` versus `Total Events` in the later files. It is not loaded. Loading it is issue #89. State rows on the annual 2020-2023 discipline files are district `State`. State rows for 2024-2026, and the 2022-23 legal file, are district `All Districts`. The published state cell is kept as published. It is not reconciled to the districts or the schools. `KYRC23_SAFE_Legal_Sanctions.csv` is the 2022-23 legal-sanctions source. Its headers match the 2024-2026 legal files (`Total`, `Arrests`, `Charges`, `Civil Proceedings`, `Court Designated Worker Involvement`, and `School Resource Officer Involvement`), so no extra mapping is used. School year `20222023` is stored as 2023. The state All Students Total is the published 4,609, and arrests are the published 190. 2019-20 through 2021-22 have no legal-sanctions source. `KYRC23_SAFE_Discipline_Resolutions.csv` republishes the historical resolutions file and is not loaded.

- `arrest_count` - Number of arrests on school property
- `charges_count` - Number of charges filed against students
- `civil_proceedings_count` - Number of civil proceedings initiated
- `corporal_punishment_count` - Number of corporal punishment incidents
- `court_designated_worker_count` - Number of court designated worker involvements
- `expelled_not_receiving_services_count` - Number of students expelled without services
- `expelled_receiving_services_count` - Number of students expelled with services provided
- `in_school_removal_count` - Number of in-school removals or in-district removals
- `out_of_school_suspension_count` - Number of out-of-school suspensions
- `removal_by_hearing_officer_count` - Number of removals ordered by hearing officer
- `restraint_count` - Number of physical restraint incidents
- `safe_schools_discipline_resolutions_students_unduplicated` - Unduplicated students with a discipline resolution, from the published `Total` on `All Students` rows only. A subgroup `Total` is not stored. A `*` is value NA with `suppressed` `Y`. A blank is not stored. A published 0 is kept
- `safe_schools_legal_sanctions_students_unduplicated` - Unduplicated students with a legal sanction, from the published `Total` on `All Students` rows only. A subgroup `Total` is not stored. A `*` is value NA with `suppressed` `Y`. A blank is not stored. A published 0 is kept
- `school_resource_officer_count` - Number of school resource officer involvements
- `seclusion_count` - Number of seclusion incidents
- `unilateral_removal_count` - Number of unilateral removals by school personnel

In 2026 the state turnover count is 4,271, the districts sum to 5,675, and the schools sum to 7,686. The likely cause is inferred from KDE's glossary wording, "left teaching in a school, district or the state": a district row counts leavers from the district, and the state row counts leavers from the state, rather than summing the schools. No KDE methodology note was found. [#68](https://github.com/jscotthorn/ky-education-kpi-pipeline/issues/68).

In 2021, 15 rows publish 100 where the turnover count is larger than the teacher count, including Virtual Academy `042020` at 0 / 1 / 100. Other years publish percents above 100. A 0% with a teacher count of 0 is a placeholder, not a real zero, and it is still stored. The 57 Kentucky Tech rows that are `N/A` in 2023 are 0/0/0 in 2022 (57 of 57). 2024 and 2025 each have 54 Kentucky Tech rows, all 0/0/0. 2020-2022 and 2024-2026 each have 92 to 103 rows with 0 teachers and a published 0%. 2023 has 39. ## Advanced Coursework
**Source**: `etl/advanced_coursework.py`

KDE publishes three measures. Participation is an unduplicated student count by demographic and course type: a student in two AP courses counts once. A completer finished at least one course. A qualifying score is a qualifying exam score on at least one AP, Cambridge, or IB exam. Courses offered is a duplicated count of course enrollments: a student in two courses counts twice. Course type `ALL` is the only user of the `advanced_*` prefix. It is the unduplicated participation total across course types, including dual credit, or the courses-offered subject total. In 2023 the state ALL enrollment is 68,238. AP + IB + Cambridge is 34,777, and that sum plus the skipped DC enrollment of 45,873 is 80,650. 68,238 sits between those two sums because a student in more than one type counts once. Course type `CAI` is Cambridge. An unrecognized course type is skipped. `advanced_offered_*` is the subject total from course type `ALL`. ## Dual Credit
**Source**: `etl/dual_credit.py`

KDE publishes two different measures. The participation file is an unduplicated student count by demographic: a student in more than one dual credit course counts once. A completer finished at least one course. A qualifying grade is a C or better in at least one course. The courses-offered file is a duplicated count of course enrollments by subject: a student in two courses counts twice. The two files do not share metric names. State All Students enrollment is 55,553 in 2026. State All Enrollments course enrollment is 143,848 in 2026.

- `dual_credit_enrollment` - Students enrolled in one or more dual credit courses

- `dual_credit_qualifying_score_count` - Students with a qualifying grade of C or better in at least one dual credit course
- `dual_credit_completion_rate` - Derived: completers divided by enrollment, times 100
- `dual_credit_qualifying_score_rate` - Derived: qualifying grades divided by completers, times 100
- `has_dual_credit_program` - 1 when enrollment is positive, suppressed Y when enrollment is starred. No registered enrollment is 0, so this flag is never 0

Courses-offered metrics (duplicated course enrollments, `student_group` is All Students, subject is in the metric name):

- `dual_credit_course_enrollment_{subject}` - Course enrollments in that subject. `all_enrollments` is the total
- `dual_credit_course_completion_count_{subject}` - Course completions in that subject
- `dual_credit_course_qualifying_score_count_{subject}` - Course qualifying grades in that subject
- `dual_credit_course_completion_rate_{subject}` - Derived: completions divided by enrollments, times 100
- `dual_credit_course_qualifying_score_rate_{subject}` - Derived: qualifying grades divided by completions, times 100

The rates are derived. KDE does not publish a rate column. There is no gap metric. Gender counts partition All Students. The race counts are within-group counts, not shares, and the published state race sum sits a few students above All Students (8, 19, 12, and 15 in 2023-2026). Overlapping groups such as economically disadvantaged are within-group counts.

There is no Suppressed column. A `*` cell is stored as value NA with suppressed Y. A blank cell is not stored. No registered file has a blank or a zero measure cell. A derived rate is omitted when either count is a star.

On the courses-offered All Enrollments enrollment, district totals equal the state in 2024, 2025, and 2026. 2023 is off by 3 (117,586 versus 117,583) with no starred district. On the participation file, the sum of district All Students enrollment is above the state in every year (165, 212, 201, and 200 students). The same rule applies to schools. A student counted at more than one school counts once on the district row and more than once in the school sum, so the school sum exceeds the district total in 11, 12, 13, and 16 districts in 2023-2026 with no starred school cell. A starred school cell is a separate gap, and the published schools then fall short of the district. Kenton County courses offered in 2026 is the large case: Scott High `291120` enrollment is starred, and the district enrollment 4,958 is 979 above the two published schools.

- Race/ethnicity groups (African American, Hispanic or Latino, etc.)
- Economically Disadvantaged
- Students with Disabilities (IEP)

- Foster Care, Gifted and Talented, Homeless, Migrant, Military Dependent

## CTE Pathways
**Source**: `etl/cte_pathways.py`

KDE publishes one row per career pathway. `Pathways Available` is the pathway name in every year, including 2018-19. The report-card glossary defines active enrollment as students enrolled during the school year in at least one valid course in that pathway. Concentrators are 12th-grade students with at least two and fewer than four credits in that pathway. Completers are 12th-grade students with four or more credits in that pathway. Those statuses are different groups. A student in two pathways is on two rows, so a sum across pathways counts that student twice. State and district rows are KDE's own pathway rows, not a sum built from the schools.

The output is one row per school, year, and student group. There is no demographic column.

### Pathway Metrics
- `num_cte_pathways` - Count of published pathway rows at the school. This is not a figure KDE publishes. A starred count still counts. A published 0 counts. A row with enrollment, concentrators, and completers all blank is not counted. Stored with `suppressed` N. 2020-2023 list some pathways under two program areas with the same counts; that pathway counts once
- `cte_pathway_enrollment` - Sum of active enrollment across pathways. A student in two pathways counts twice. Value NA with `suppressed` Y when any pathway's enrollment is `*`. A partial sum is not stored
- `cte_concentrator_count` - Sum of concentrators across pathways, with the same star rule. A student in two pathways counts twice. Not emitted for 2019, because that file has no concentrator column. The 2018-19 preparatory-student column is a different measure and is not stored. Concentrators appear to be counted across grades
- `cte_pathway_completer_count` - Sum of completers across pathways, with the same star rule. A student in two pathways counts twice. This is not CTE Participation's `cte_completer_count_grade_12`, which counts each 12th-grade completer once. In 2026 the published state pathway-completer cells sum to at least 36,436. The participation state count is 19,990
- `cte_pathway_completion_rate` - Pipeline-calculated: completers divided by concentrators, times 100, rounded to 1 decimal place. KDE does not publish this rate. Scott approved it on 2026-10-19 as an existing-practice exception. KDE's concentrator group (at least two and fewer than four credits) and completer group (four or more credits) do not overlap, so the rate is not a share of concentrators. Any starred pathway suppresses it. 2019 has none because that file has no concentrator column. Concentrators appear to be counted across grades

**Note**: `student_group` is `All Students` on every row. KYRC state rows are district `All Districts` and school id `999000` (the school name `All Schools` is stored as `---District Total---`). 2019-2023 state rows are district `State` and school id `999`. In 2020-2026 the smallest published count is 3, so a star can also mean 0-2 or not applicable. 2019 publishes 0 and has no stars. Those zeros stay. 386 all-zero school pathway rows raise the 2019 school pathway-count mean to 12.04; without those rows it is 10.75. Later years sit between 10.4 and 11.4. The 2026 state pathway count is 113. The 2026 state enrollment, concentrator, and completer totals are suppressed because 9, 6, and 15 pathway cells are `*`. On KYRC25 and KYRC26, some state pathway cells do not equal the sum of the district cells, and the district sum is higher. Each level keeps the value KDE published. That difference is flagged kde-recheck in [issue #86](https://github.com/jscotthorn/ky-education-kpi-pipeline/issues/86). District `606` (Model Laboratory Schools at EKU) is new in 2026. Caldwell County High School `081010` is enrollment 556, concentrators 413, completers 143, and a calculated completion rate of 34.6.

**Data Sources**: `CAREER_PATHWAYS_18-19.xlsx`, `career_pathways_2020.csv` through `career_pathways_2023.csv`, `KYRC24_CTE_Career_Pathways.csv`, `KYRC25_CTE_Career_Pathways.csv`, `KYRC26_CTE_Career_Pathways.csv`
**Years Available**: 2019-2026
**Unit**: Count (non-negative integers). `num_cte_pathways` is a count of published pathway rows. Enrollment, concentrators, and completers are sums across pathways. The completion rate is completers / concentrators * 100, calculated by the pipeline and rounded to 1 decimal place
**Demographics**: School-level only (All Students)

Total graduates is published down to 13 in KYRC25 (one starred district) and 10 in KYRC26 (no star). Where every district cell is a number, the state cell still does not equal the sum of the districts. Each level keeps the value KDE published.

The older names `career_readiness_cooperative_education_count`, `career_readiness_internship_count`, `career_readiness_advanced_placement_count`, and `career_readiness_transition_readiness_count` had no source column on any registered file and are not emitted. Co-op and internship are the work-based learning count. Advanced Placement is academic readiness and is not in this file.

## District School List (School Coordinates and Institutional Characteristics)
**Source**: `etl/district_school_list.py`

Geographic coordinates and institutional classification data for Kentucky schools.

### Geographic Coordinates
- `school_latitude` - School latitude in decimal degrees (approximately 36-39° for Kentucky)
- `school_longitude` - School longitude in decimal degrees (approximately -89° to -82° for Kentucky)

### School Type (Dummy-Encoded)
KDE school classification codes, dummy-encoded for use in Bayesian models. A1 (standard public school) is the reference category (all dummies = 0).

- `school_type_a2` - Career/Technical Education center (1=yes, 0=no)
- `school_type_a3` - Special education program (1=yes, 0=no)
- `school_type_a4` - Preschool program (1=yes, 0=no)
- `school_type_a5` - Alternative program - remediation (1=yes, 0=no)
- `school_type_a6` - Alternative program - state agency children (1=yes, 0=no)
- `school_type_a8` - Other classification A8 (1=yes, 0=no)
- `school_type_b1` - Classification B1 (1=yes, 0=no)
- `school_type_b2` - Classification B2 (1=yes, 0=no)
- `school_type_c2` - Classification C2 (1=yes, 0=no)
- `school_type_d1` - Classification D1 (1=yes, 0=no)

### Title I Status (Dummy-Encoded)
Title I federal funding status, dummy-encoded for use in Bayesian models. "Not a Title 1 School" is the reference category (all dummies = 0).

- `title_i_schoolwide` - Title I Schoolwide program (1=yes, 0=no) - includes Eligible-Schoolwide, Schoolwide School, and Eligible-Schoolwide Program
- `title_i_targeted` - Title I Targeted Assistance program (1=yes, 0=no)
- `title_i_eligible_no_program` - Title I Eligible but no program implemented (1=yes, 0=no)

**Note**: This dataset contains school-level data only. All records have `student_group='All Students'`. There is no demographic column and no suppression column. A `*` does not appear. `suppressed` is N on every row. District aggregate rows (`---District Total---` through 2025, `---District---` in 2026) are not stored. The state row is stored. Its district is `State` for 2019-2025 and `---State---` for 2026. The school id is `999` for 2019-2023 and `999000` for 2024-2026. The 2026 state coordinate is 38.184805, -84.848799, the same point as the earlier years. Jefferson County's co-op was blank in KYRC25 and is `GLEC` in KYRC26. `GRREC` is unchanged. A blank coordinate is not stored. A blank grade is not stored as 0. `Adult Ed` and `Sp Ed` are not numeric grades.

## Naming Conventions

All KPIs follow these standard naming patterns:
- **Rates**: `{indicator}_rate_{period}` (values 0-100)
- **Counts**: `{indicator}_count_{period}` (non-negative integers)
- **Totals**: `{indicator}_total_{period}` (denominators for rate calculations)

Grade levels are normalized to: `kindergarten`, `grade_1`, `grade_2`, etc.
Education levels include: `elementary`, `middle`, `high`
