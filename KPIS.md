## Out-of-School Suspension
**Source**: `etl/out_of_school_suspension.py`

KDE's report-card glossary defines `OVW_Student_Suspensions` as Student Suspensions (CRDC): counts of in-school and out-of-school suspensions for students with and without disabilities, from the biennial Civil Rights Data Collection. Single means one out-of-school suspension. Multiple means more than one. These are student counts.

**The KPI year is the report-card year (`School Year`), not the CRDC collected year.** Report-card year 2026 is the 2021-22 collection (`Collected School Year` `20212022` on every KYRC26 row). Report-card year 2024 is the 2020-21 collection (`Collected School Year` `20202021` on every KYRC24 row). The output has no collected-year column.

`KYRC25_OVW_Student_Suspensions.csv` is the same 2020-21 collection with `School Year` `20242025`. It is not registered. These files are school rows only. There is no `999000` state row and no district total.

A total is suppressed when any component is suppressed. Summing the published parts is not the file's count, including the case where every part is starred and the sum of the remaining parts is 0.

Annual Safe Schools `OUT OF SCHOOL SUSPENSION SSP3` counts are not emitted here. `etl/safe_schools_discipline.py` publishes `out_of_school_suspension_count` and the other resolution counts.

### CRDC metrics (report-card years 2024 and 2026)
- `out_of_school_suspension_single_with_disabilities_count` - Students with disabilities with one out-of-school suspension
- `out_of_school_suspension_single_without_disabilities_count` - Students without disabilities with one out-of-school suspension
- `out_of_school_suspension_multiple_with_disabilities_count` - Students with disabilities with more than one out-of-school suspension
- `out_of_school_suspension_multiple_without_disabilities_count` - Students without disabilities with more than one out-of-school suspension
- `out_of_school_suspension_single_total_count` - Single-suspension students, both disability groups. Suppressed when either component is suppressed
- `out_of_school_suspension_multiple_total_count` - Multiple-suspension students, both disability groups. Suppressed when either component is suppressed
- `out_of_school_suspension_total_count` - Students with an out-of-school suspension (single plus multiple). Suppressed when any component is suppressed
- `in_school_suspension_with_disabilities_count` - In-school suspensions for students with disabilities
- `in_school_suspension_without_disabilities_count` - In-school suspensions for students without disabilities
- `in_school_suspension_total_count` - In-school suspensions, both disability groups. Suppressed when either component is suppressed

## Secondary Enrollment
**Source**: `etl/secondary_enrollment.py`

Students with a secondary (concurrent) enrollment at this school, by grade. KDE's cover sheet describes the total as "Count of unique students with secondary enrollment". Our interpretation of the data is a concurrent second enrollment at a school other than the student's main school, mostly at career and technical centers and regional academies. KDE pairs this series with primary enrollment.

All Grades counts unique students in every grade. KYRC files publish only grades 6-12 and 14, so All Grades can exceed the sum of those cells. Older files also had preschool through grade 5 columns, and those cells are 0 or blank (2019 has blanks). For 2019-2024 the All Grades total equals the sum of the published grade cells. KYRC25 has 37 rows where it does not (state gap 3). KYRC26 has 47 rows (state gap 63: East Jessamine Middle 61, Tates Creek Middle 1, Camden Station Elementary 1). Those students are in grades the file does not publish; cause unverified. 62 of the 63 are at East Jessamine Middle and Tates Creek Middle, both grades 6-8 schools. On those rows, middle + high school + grade 14 will not equal the total. The parser stores All Grades.

## Teacher Experience
**Source**: `etl/teacher_experience.py`

Average years of teaching experience for classroom teachers at the school or district. KDE's report-card glossary calls this Average School Experience: the total years of teaching experience divided by the number of teachers. The 2018-19 workbook cover sheet describes the average as "Average number of years' experience by teachers" and the educator count as the teachers who contribute to that total. 703 KAR 5:140 limits the average to classroom teachers and excludes administrators, guidance counselors, and media specialists. It is not the number of years a teacher has worked in that building.

The parser stores the published average. The educator count is the denominator in the source file and is not a separate KPI. There is no demographic breakdown and no Suppressed column. A school with one teacher publishes its average.

- `teacher_average_years_experience` - Average years of teaching experience for classroom teachers at the school

**Data Sources**: KYRC26_OVW_Average_Years_School_Experience.csv, KYRC25_OVW_Average_Years_School_Experience.csv, KYRC24_OVW_Average_Years_School_Experience.csv, average_years_school_experience_{year}.csv, SCHOOL_EXPERIENCE_18-19.xlsx  
**Years Available**: 2019-2026 (2019 when the workbook can be read)  
**Unit**: Years

## Student-Teacher Ratio
**Source**: `etl/student_teacher_ratio.py`

Students per one teacher at the school. KDE's report-card glossary defines Student to Teacher Ratio as 6/30 SAAR membership divided by full-time-equivalent teachers, excluding administrators, guidance counselors, media specialists, and resource teachers. 703 KAR 5:140 calls it the average student/teacher ratio: end-of-year membership divided by FTE teachers, excluding administrators, guidance counselors, and media specialists. The 2018-19 cover sheet describes the field as the number of students enrolled compared with the number of teachers on staff.

The parser stores the published ratio, the number before the colon in `15:01`. It does not recompute membership divided by FTE. There is no demographic breakdown and no Suppressed column. KYRC26 publishes school rows only. It does not include the state row or the district totals, and this pipeline does not rebuild them. The glossary excludes the Kentucky School for the Blind, the Kentucky School for the Deaf, and the Model Laboratory schools from the state total.

- `student_teacher_ratio` - Students per one teacher (15.0 is the published ratio `15:01`)

**Data Sources**: KYRC26_OVW_Student_to_Teacher_Ratio.csv, KYRC25_OVW_Student_to_Teacher_Ratio.csv, KYRC24_OVW_Student_to_Teacher_Ratio.csv, student_teacher_ratio_{year}.csv, STUDENT_TEACHER_RATIO_18-19.xlsx  
**Years Available**: 2019-2026 (2019 when the workbook can be read)  
**Unit**: Students per teacher

## Novice Teachers
**Source**: `etl/novice_teachers.py`

KDE's report-card glossary defines an inexperienced teacher as a teacher with 0-3 years of teaching experience. A teacher in the first year (less than 1 year) is a New Teacher. The 2021-22 report-card change note splits that group into less than one year and between one and three years. The quality-assurance worksheet says the counts come from the Kentucky Educator Credentialing System. The students-taught file is the comparison of students taught by those teachers.

The parser stores the two published teacher rates separately. It does not add them into one total. The 2018-19 workbook cover sheet calls its rate the percentage of new teachers, so 2019 emits only the less-than-1-year rate. A blank percent on that file, with zero new teachers and a positive teacher count, is stored as 0.0. The 2020 file has a new-teacher count and no rate. The 2021 file has one combined inexperienced-teacher rate (`PERCENT INEXPIENCED TEACHERS`). Those columns are left unmapped so they are not written as either split rate.

The registered files have no Suppressed column and no starred cell. A published rate of 0 is kept. The equity gap is published two ways. 2020-2023 and KYRC26 publish a signed gap (Title I minus Not Title I). KYRC23, KYRC24, and KYRC25 publish the absolute value. The parser still drops a negative cell, so the signed years lose those gaps. That behavior is unchanged here. [Issue #59](https://github.com/jscotthorn/ky-education-kpi-pipeline/issues/59) tracks storing a signed gap for every year. [Issue #58](https://github.com/jscotthorn/ky-education-kpi-pipeline/issues/58) tracks the registered KYRC24 equity file against the datasets copy. State rows use district `All Districts` for 2024-2026 and district `State` for 2019-2023.

### Institutional Metrics
- `novice_teacher_rate_less_than_1_year` - Percentage of teachers with less than 1 year of experience (a New Teacher)
- `novice_teacher_rate_1_to_3_years` - Percentage of teachers with 1-3 years of experience

### Equity Metrics (Students Taught by Inexperienced Teachers)
- `students_taught_by_inexperienced_teachers_rate_title_1` - Percentage of students taught by inexperienced teachers in Title I schools
- `students_taught_by_inexperienced_teachers_rate_not_title_1` - Percentage of students taught by inexperienced teachers in non-Title I schools
- `students_taught_by_inexperienced_teachers_rate_equity_gap` - Equity gap. 2020-2023 and KYRC26 publish Title I minus Not Title I, and a negative cell is dropped ([#59](https://github.com/jscotthorn/ky-education-kpi-pipeline/issues/59)). KYRC23, KYRC24, and KYRC25 publish the absolute value. The registered KYRC24 copy is the historical file ([#58](https://github.com/jscotthorn/ky-education-kpi-pipeline/issues/58))

**Demographics (via `student_group` column):**
- All Students
- White (non-Hispanic) / Non-White
- Economically Disadvantaged / Non-Economically Disadvantaged
- Students with Disabilities (IEP) / Student without Disabilities (IEP)
- English Learner / Non-English Learner

**Data Sources**: KYRC26_OVW_Inexperienced_Teachers.csv, KYRC26_OVW_Students_Taught_by_Inexperienced_Teachers.csv, the KYRC25 and KYRC24 twins, inexperienced_teachers_{year}.csv, students_taught_by_inexperienced_teachers_{year}.csv, NEW_TEACHER_COUNT_18-19.xlsx
**Years Available**: 2019-2026 for the first-year teacher rate (2019 when the workbook can be read); 2022-2026 for the 1-3 year rate; 2020-2026 for the student-equity rates. 2020 and 2021 have no institutional rate rows
**Unit**: Rate (0-100). The stored equity gap is non-negative. Signed years (2020-2023 and KYRC26) drop negatives. The registered 2024 and 2025 files publish the absolute value and are stored as published

## Teacher Turnover
**Source**: `etl/teacher_turnover.py`

School-level teacher turnover. KDE's 2018-19 workbook cover sheet defines the percent as the percentage of teacher turnover experienced by the school or district, the turnover count as the number of teaching positions which experienced turnover, and the teacher count as the total number of teachers. There is no demographic column. The ETL stores the published percent and does not rebuild it from the counts.

KYRC26 district and state rows are a different measure from 2019-2025. Do not read them as the next point in that series. From 2019 through 2025, state turnover equals the sum of district turnover. The 2025 state turnover count is 10,612. 2020 is the exception: state turnover is 8,154 and the districts sum to 8,133, off by 21, and the Kentucky Tech district row (`777`) is 0/0/0. In 2026 the state turnover count is 4,271, the districts sum to 5,675, and the schools sum to 7,686. District turnover is below that district's school sum in 151 of 177 districts (0 districts in 2025). Teacher counts in 2026 are state 40,557, districts 40,639, schools 42,406. ## Naming Conventions

All KPIs follow these standard naming patterns:
- **Rates**: `{indicator}_rate_{period}` (values 0-100)
- **Counts**: `{indicator}_count_{period}` (non-negative integers)
- **Totals**: `{indicator}_total_{period}` (denominators for rate calculations)

Grade levels are normalized to: `kindergarten`, `grade_1`, `grade_2`, etc.
Education levels include: `elementary`, `middle`, `high`
