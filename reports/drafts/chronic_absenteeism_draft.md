## Indicator Overview

Chronic Absenteeism measures the percentage of students who miss 10% or more of school days. Regular attendance is critical for academic success and student engagement.

Students who miss 10% of yearly school time (about two days a month) for any reason, excused or unexcused, are considered chronically absent. This measure helps identify students who may need extra support to stay engaged in school.

**Note:** For this indicator, **lower values indicate better performance.**
