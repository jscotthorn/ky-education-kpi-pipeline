## Indicator Overview

9th Grade On-Track measures the percentage of 9th graders who meet promotion criteria by the end of the school year, meaning they have earned enough credits to be on track toward graduation.

Students who fall off-track in 9th grade are significantly less likely to graduate on time. This indicator is tracked for Fayette County high schools only.

## Key Observations and Recommendations

FCPS defines a student as "9th grade on-track" if they earn a minimum of 5.5 credits AND received a Math credit AND received an English credit, making them “on track” to graduate in four years. This is a locally-developed measure — Kentucky does not currently have a formal statewide definition for 9th grade on-track status or include it in the state report card, making FCPS one of the relatively few districts in the state systematically tracking this leading indicator of graduation. In 2024-25, 84% of FCPS 9th graders met this benchmark. FCPS has invested in multiple programs to support successful high school transitions, including Freshman Academies at Bryan Station, Tates Creek, and Frederick Douglass — small learning communities that provide dedicated counselors, team-based teaching, and a required Freshman Seminar covering study skills, goal setting, and career exploration — as well as the Summer Ignite Transition Week, which orients incoming 9th graders to their new school before the year begins. See https://www.fcps.net/academic-programs/academies-of-lexington for more information.

English Learners face the largest on-track gap of any group: just 67% of EL students met the on-track threshold in 2024-25, 17 percentage points below the district average, and this gap has remained essentially flat over the past three years (-0.8 pp). Students with Disabilities (IEP) and Hispanic or Latino students also trail the district average (78.5% and 75.4% respectively), though both groups are improving — Students with Disabilities gained 4.9 percentage points over three years, the strongest trend of any demographic group. For students who do fall off-track, FCPS offers credit recovery through eSchool (an online platform available year-round) and the Re-engagement Center at Success Academy, which serves students ages 16+ who are at least one year behind in credits and need a flexible, accelerated pathway to graduation. See https://success.fcps.net/re-engagement-center for more information.
