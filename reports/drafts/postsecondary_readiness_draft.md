## Indicator Overview

Postsecondary Readiness measures whether students are prepared for college or career after high school. This includes meeting benchmarks in academics, career exploration, and college preparation.

Students must earn a diploma and meet at least one readiness measure, such as college entrance exam scores, dual credit courses, AP/IB exams, industry certifications, or approved work experiences.
