# 105 — Teacher working conditions, one row per Impact Kentucky administration

PR #105. Refs #62. These notes moved out of `notes/104--kyrc26-file-to-etl-mapping.md` before review. `KPIS.md` and `README.md` are unchanged from `feature/2025-26-kyrc26` at `18f4a675dbe20120718c5f448b2351166780ca42`. Update those after merge.

Each Impact Kentucky administration is stored once. The KPI year is the ending year of the school year in which the survey was administered. That is the same rule `BaseETL.extract_year` uses everywhere else: an 8-digit `YYYYYYYY` school year keeps the last four digits. Years with no survey are absent. The metrics are not renamed and Teaching Environment is not copied onto School Climate.

| Survey | Administration | School year | KPI year | File kept | Not stored as its own year |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2019-20 Impact Kentucky (the 2020 survey; first Impact administration) | January 13-February 21, 2020 | 2019-20 | 2020 | `teacher_working_conditions_2020.csv` (`20192020` on all 4,686 rows) | `teacher_working_conditions_2021.csv` (`20202021`) |
| 2021-22 Impact Kentucky (the 2022 survey) | November 1-December 17, 2021 | 2021-22 | 2022 | `teacher_working_conditions_2022.csv` (`20212022` on all 4,635 rows) | `teacher_working_conditions_2023.csv` (`20222023`, plus three Breathitt rows typed `20022023`) and `KYRC23_OVW_Teacher_Working_Conditions.csv` |
| 2023-24 Impact Kentucky | November 1-December 15, 2023 | 2023-24 | 2024 | datasets `KYRC24_OVW_Teacher_Working_Conditions.csv` (`20232024`, 4,305 rows), plus Craft Academy from the historical file (6 rows) and `777135` from KYRC25 (3 rows) | the rest of the historical KYRC24 file and the rest of `KYRC25_OVW_Teacher_Working_Conditions.csv` (`20242025`) |
| 2025-26 Impact Kentucky | November 10-December 19, 2025 | 2025-26 | 2026 | `KYRC26_OVW_Teacher_Working_Conditions.csv` (`20252026`) | none |

Evidence for those windows: KDE advisory 20-151 (June 1, 2020) and impactky.org say Panorama administered the inaugural survey January 13-February 21, 2020, with more than 43,000 responses. The October 14, 2020 School Report Card release says those 2020 results are on the 2019-20 report card. The NKyTribune (November 1, 2021) and the 2022 impactky.org survey page say the second administration was November 1-December 17, 2021. Kentucky Teacher (February 2024) says the next administration was November 1-December 15, 2023. KDE advisory 26-25 (February 2, 2026) says the 2025-26 survey was administered November 10-December 19, 2025, and gives statewide percent favorable of 69 for managing student behavior, 67 for school climate, and 70 for school leadership. Those three numbers are the KYRC26 state row. A KSBA executive briefing lists the administrations as 2019-20, 2021-22, and 2023-24, with no survey in the in-between school years. Go Teach KY says the survey is every other year and the next window after 2023-24 was fall 2025.

Shared school-and-construct keys, compared as a multiset of percents so Glasgow's row order does not count as a difference: 2020 versus 2021, 4,629 shared keys, 0 differences. 2022 versus 2023, 4,617 shared keys, 0 differences. KYRC24 historical versus KYRC25, 3,672 shared keys, 9 differences, all district totals. The datasets copy of KYRC24 matches KYRC25 on all 4,299 shared keys and is the 2024 source. Where the historical file disagrees, the datasets percent is stored. The historical file and KYRC25 add only keys the datasets file does not already supply (issue #95).

`accepts_data_file` skips `teacher_working_conditions_2021.csv`, `teacher_working_conditions_2023.csv`, and `KYRC23_OVW_Teacher_Working_Conditions.csv` by exact filename. KYRC23 also turns 945 `N/A` cells into 0 and relabels Teaching Environment as School Climate. The historical KYRC24 object is downloaded as `KYRC24_OVW_Teacher_Working_Conditions_historical.csv` (`save_as` in `config/kde_sources.yaml`) so it does not overwrite the datasets file. The datasets file is registered with `url: kyrc25`, which is the datasets container, not the school year.
