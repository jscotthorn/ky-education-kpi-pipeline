# KDE guidance sources

This list is the public KDE material used to flag decision records. It is not a decision. Flags do not change a recorded choice.

The 25 October 2026 research attachment supplied the URLs and most of the quotations. This pass re-fetched two of those pages and the quotations matched:

- [CHG-2022](https://www.education.ky.gov/districts/tech/sis/Documents/SRC_ReportingChanges_2022.pdf), "Changes to Reporting Components on the 2021-2022 Kentucky School Report Card", 10 October 2022. Re-fetched 25 October 2026. The suppression paragraph, "Counts are no longer available", "CTE Participation/Participation Completers is new", "Current Year Graduates is new", and the spring financial-transparency sentence are in that PDF.
- [REL-2026](https://content.govdelivery.com/accounts/KYDE/bulletins/42e02ae), 15 October 2026 release. Re-fetched 25 October 2026. It states the 4-year graduation rate 94.1% and the 5-year rate 94.5%, that 2025-26 is the final year of the current accountability system, that SAT results should not be compared with ACT results, and that 2025-26 is the last year the school climate and safety indicator is in the accountability system.

Other KDE quotations below are from that research attachment and were not re-fetched in this pass. Repo claims that the research made about this pipeline were checked against the current `feature/2025-26-kyrc26` tree. Those checks are in the decision records.

## What was not found

The research looked for these and did not find a KDE statement. This pass did not repeat that search.

- A KDE "changes to reporting components" document for 2018-19, 2019-20, 2023-24, 2024-25, or 2025-26. The research says the same URL pattern used for CHG-2021, CHG-2022, and CHG-2023 returns 404 for 2019, 2020, 2024, 2025, and 2026.
- A KDE notice for the May 2026 republish of the KYRC24 CTE file, or for any other dated republish of that file.
- A KDE definition of a blank cell, of `--`, of `N/A`, or of a 0.0 rate published next to a blank count.
- A KDE statement that `*` means "not applicable", "no students", or row padding.
- A KDE notice announcing the change from state `999` / district `State` to state `999000` / district `All Districts`, or a crosswalk from the old IDs to the new ones.
- A KDE note that the 2023 CTE completion denominator changed, or that names what each KYRC24 CTE column contains.
- A KDE note announcing that KYRC25 and KYRC26 CTE participation files are counts only, or saying whether the dashboard still shows a participation or completion rate.
- A KDE statement that 2020 Teaching Environment and later School Climate are the same measure, or that they are not.
- KDE guidance on whether a CRDC row's KPI year should be `School Year` or `Collected School Year`.

`notes/103--2025-26-kyrc26-data-update-plan.md` already says the 10-day regulatory review runs through 26 October 2026 and that both containers should be re-HEAD'd after that date. The research cites SRC13 and SRC14 for the clock time "Oct. 26 at 5 p.m. ET". Those two PDFs were not re-fetched in this pass. The 5 p.m. ET time is therefore the research's citation, not a quote verified here. The date 26 October 2026 is also in notes/103.
