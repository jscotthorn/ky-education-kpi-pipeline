# Republishes and alternate copies

This is the place [0052](0052-republish-supersedes-earlier.md) requires for differences on each republish. The rule is: use the latest public KDE file after the review window closes, unless that copy is visibly broken, and record the differences here.

**Complies with 0052?** means the registered copy follows that rule. "Not yet conformed" means this pass did not find that the registration matches, and the row is not treated as exempt. Issue [#123](https://github.com/jscotthorn/ky-education-kpi-pipeline/issues/123) tracks the Open House copies that still do not apply the 18 October choices on #58, #64, and #66. The rewritten 2024-25 files (R19 and R20) are compared in [republish-diffs/kyrc25-2026-rewrites.md](republish-diffs/kyrc25-2026-rewrites.md) (pull request [#124](https://github.com/jscotthorn/ky-education-kpi-pipeline/pull/124)). R19 complies. R20 complies for postsecondary readiness, 4-year graduation, and 5-year graduation.

The 26 rows are from the 25 October 2026 research attachment. Cell counts, hashes, and Last-Modified times in the "What differs" column were not re-counted in this pass, except where the "Checked here" column says the config was re-read. KDE quotations in that research were not re-fetched. Sources for the KDE side are listed under the table.
